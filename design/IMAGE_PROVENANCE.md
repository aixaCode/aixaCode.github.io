# Image provenance and production sources

Recovered hero, portrait, seven unchanged editorial illustrations and their original variants come from the existing CT 126 deployment. Original artist/generator and rights history were not recovered; this migration does not infer them.

The improvement-plan v4 illustration was generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 5 October 2026, using its v3 illustration only as a style reference. Original output is tracked in design/originals/improvement-plan-v4.png. Published WebP variants are in site/assets/images/make-improvement-plans-winnable-editorial-v4*.webp.

Prompt: create one 3:2 landscape editorial illustration; warm ivory paper, fine charcoal-brown imperfect pencil outlines and subtle pale terracotta, sage and muted-blue watercolour. Replace the checklist subject with two simplified human hands collaboratively placing planning cards in a shared open notebook. Connect observation, conversation, support, a work opportunity and follow-up. Emphasise shared planning and opportunity. No words, scores, red crosses, ticks, pass/fail rows or surveillance.

All diagram v4 SVGs are generated by scripts/render-diagrams.py from design/diagrams.json, with a shared cream/charcoal/pastel palette and readable DM Sans labels. Recovered diagram files remain available as provenance and for already cached pages. The bundled font license is preserved.

Nine v4 social cards use original artwork unaltered inside a native SVG layout, generated by scripts/render-social-cards.mjs. Editable source templates live in design/social-cards/; published 1200x630 JPEGs live in site/assets/images/. Text comes from content/essays.json. Exporting artwork variants and rendering the template uses Sharp; no original image is overwritten.

The 7 October polish versions four diagram families as v5 with explicit grouped
branch paths; nodes and semantic edges are preserved. The prioritization v5 share
card uses a reviewed three-line title in design/social-layouts.json to prevent
artwork covering its last letters. Artwork is unchanged. Other v4 cards remain.

The 28 October 2026 v5 covers for seven essays and the draft agents essay were
generated in Codex with its built-in image tool, using the improvement-plan v4
illustration as the style reference and the prompts in design/cover-prompts-v5.md.
Originals are in design/originals/*-v5.png. Published WebP variants are
site/assets/images/<slug>-editorial-v5*.webp; their share cards are the v6 JPEGs
rendered by scripts/render-social-cards.mjs. The v3 covers and older cards they
replace were removed.
