# Essay cover prompts (v5)

Goal: every essay cover in the same warm, illustrated notebook style as the
improvement-plan v4 cover (`design/originals/improvement-plan-v4.png`). Attach
that image as the style reference with each prompt.

Output: one 3:2 landscape PNG per essay (1536x1024 or larger). Save as
`design/originals/<slug>-v5.png`; the build step makes the WebP sizes and share
cards.

## Shared style (paste before each prompt)

> One 3:2 landscape editorial illustration, matching the attached reference
> exactly in style: warm ivory paper background, fine charcoal-brown imperfect
> pencil outlines, soft watercolour in pale terracotta, sage, muted blue and
> butter yellow. Seen from above on a desk. Simplified hands with sleeves, an
> open notebook, a coffee cup, a small potted plant, a pencil and sticky notes
> may appear. Calm, generous white space. No words, letters, numbers, logos,
> brand names, screens with readable text, scores, red crosses or ticks.

## 1. AI Makes Building Easy. Production Is Hard.
`ai-makes-building-easy-production-is-hard`

> Subject: on the left, a quick pile of paper prototypes and sketch cards being
> produced easily by a small sparkle-shaped helper; on the right, the same card
> being carefully checked by human hands with a magnifying glass, a small
> shield and a checklist card before it is placed on a sturdy shelf labelled
> only with a simple house icon. The contrast is fast making versus careful
> owning.

## 2. Before You Remove Code Review, Name the Risk It Controls
`before-you-remove-code-review-name-the-risk`

> Subject: a single gate in the middle of the notebook with four small paths
> behind it, each marked only by an icon (shield, bug, people, compass). Two
> hands sort change cards into the right path instead of all through one gate.
> A small sticky note with a question-mark doodle sits beside it.

## 3. A Career Ladder Should Reduce Surprises, Not Create Bureaucracy
`career-ladder-should-reduce-surprises`

> Subject: a simple wooden ladder drawn across the notebook pages, with small
> example cards (a conversation bubble, a small project, a helping hand)
> clipped to each rung. One person's hand points to the next rung while a
> second hand offers a card. No checklist grid, no forms.

## 4. Feedback Is the Performance System
`feedback-is-the-performance-system`

> Subject: two people's hands across the desk exchanging small sticky notes
> often, a gentle looping arrow drawn between them, and a tiny calendar with
> many small marks rather than one big annual date. A coffee cup for each
> person. Warm and conversational.

## 5. Prioritization Is Constraint Management
`prioritization-is-constraint-management`

> Subject: a funnel or narrow pipe drawn in the notebook where many idea cards
> wait above it; one hand widens the narrow neck with a pencil while the other
> sets most cards aside in a neat "later" pile. A small hourglass nearby.

## 6. Technical Direction Is Not a Technology Decision
`technical-direction-is-not-a-technology-decision`

> Subject: a compass and a simple hand-drawn map with a flag destination on one
> page; on the other page, small tool cards (gear, cloud, cube) waiting.
> One hand places the flag first, then the other picks a tool card that points
> toward it.

## 7. Use AI to Find Evidence, Not Score Engineers
`use-ai-to-find-evidence-not-score-engineers`

> Subject: a small sparkle-shaped helper gathering scattered cards (a speech
> bubble, a code bracket, a small document) into a tidy folder, each card tied
> with a thin thread back to where it came from. Two human hands open the
> folder together to look. No stars, scores, gauges or rankings.

## 8. AI Agents Write the Code. I Still Own What Done Means. (PR #12)
`ai-agents-write-the-code-i-own-done`

> Subject: one hand holding a pencil over the notebook, which shows a simple
> board of task cards; several small friendly sparkle-shaped helpers carry
> cards between columns. At the end of the board sits a single card with a
> small flag that only the human hand is touching. A coffee cup and a plant at
> the edges.
