ABOUT / AGNIESZKA BESZ

Technical depth.
A wider perspective.

I lead engineering teams and I still build. Most recently VP of Engineering at Decentraland, now building through GB Factory.

Engineering leadership,
rooted in doing the work.

I have spent more than 15 years in game development, across PC, web, iOS, Android and VR. I have shipped games with Ubisoft, Next Games and Rovio, including South Park: Phone Destroyer, The Walking Dead: No Man's Land and Stranger Things: Puzzle Tales. At Rovio I led Angry Birds Journey from concept to soft launch.

At Decentraland I started on the original web client, then led the ground-up rebuild and production release of its Unity/C# PC client. As VP of Engineering until June 2026, my scope covered client engineering, Creator Tools, SDK and QA, with up to 25 developers in 10 countries. Before that, I grew a technology department from 25 to 50 people at Ten Square Games.

Now I build through GB Factory: Animal Park, my own mobile game; Luke, the family assistant I run at home; and client work. Most of it runs through coordinated AI task sessions, and I still decide what ships.

I am open to engineering leadership roles, advisory work and product builds.
